MINDFUL TOGETHER — MENTAL HEALTH ADVOCACY WEBSITE
=================================================

PAGES
- index.html   Home page and community advocacy introduction
- privacy.html Privacy Statement
- contact.html Name, Email, Message contact form
- style.css    Blue-and-white styling, responsive layout, fonts, and icons
- script.js    Mobile navigation and demo form feedback

HOW TO OPEN
1. Download and extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a web browser.
3. Use the navigation links to visit all three pages.

DESIGN FEATURES
- Blue, white, and pale-blue color palette
- Nunito headings and DM Sans body text (Google Fonts)
- Community and mental health awareness imagery from Unsplash
- Heart and support icons, responsive navigation, accessible form labels

IMPORTANT NOTES
- The contact form is only a front-end demonstration. It does not email, transmit, or save submissions.
- The privacy statement explains that the website does not intentionally use tracking cookies or analytics, and notes that external image/font providers may receive standard technical information.
- Internet access is needed to load external photos and fonts.
- This is an awareness project, not a counseling or medical service.
