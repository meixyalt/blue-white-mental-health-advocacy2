YOUR TRUST MATTERS

Privacy, with
care and clarity.

This statement explains what information this student advocacy website collects and how the sample contact form works.

01

Information collection

This website is an informational community advocacy project. It does not operate its own analytics, advertising trackers, or visitor database. The contact form is a front-end demonstration and does not transmit or save names, email addresses, or messages.

02

Cookies and tracking

The site does not intentionally set tracking cookies or use tracking scripts. Images are loaded from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. Those third-party services may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads their content. Review their privacy policies for more details.

03

Sensitive information

Please do not enter private health details or other sensitive personal information into the sample form. This website is an awareness project, not a counseling service, and it does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

04

Future changes

If the form is connected to email, analytics, or a database in the future, this statement must be updated to describe the information collected, its purpose, who can access it, and how it is protected.

Last updated: October 2026 · This is a privacy statement for a student-project demonstration website.
